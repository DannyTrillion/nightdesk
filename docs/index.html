The primitive

A third option

Instead of a binary fresh-or-stale check, NightDesk returns a confidence score that decays at a rate set by the session. An hour-old price during the open is suspect, because discovery is happening without it. The same price at 2am on a Sunday is the best information in existence. A lending market reads that number and widens its parameters instead of freezing.

What you call

(int256 price,
uint256 age,
uint16 confidenceBps,

What it replaces

// the documented advice
(, int256 p,, uint256 u,) = feed.latestRoundData();
require(block.timestamp - u < 86400);
// ...and on a Sunday, you revert

The half a contract can't do alone

A clock knows the calendar. It does not know that a market holiday cancelled today's session, or that something happened overnight that makes Friday's close a bad estimate. The agent supplies both, and the contract fails closed to zero confidence if the agent stops reporting.

How session state is derived

A hardcoded holiday calendar rots within a year. Instead the agent cross-checks a New York clock against what the feeds are actually doing. If the calendar claims a trading session but every feed has been silent for two hours, the market is closed — holiday, outage, it doesn't matter. Same handling, no maintenance.
